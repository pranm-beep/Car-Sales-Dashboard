Car Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project

Project Overview

The Car Sales Dashboard is an interactive data visualization
project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It aims to analyze
and present car sales performance through charts, reports,
and key performance indicators (KPIs).

This dashboard provides insights into total car sales,
customer information, sales revenue, company performance,
dealer regions, dealer performance, customer gender
distribution, and car body styles.

It transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual
information that helps users understand sales trends,
identify top-performing dealers, and evaluate business
performance.

Project Objectives

- Analyze overall car sales performance.
- Monitor total sales revenue and cars sold.
- Identify sales performance by car company.
- Compare sales across different dealer regions.
- Determine top-performing dealers.
- Analyze customer distribution by gender.
- Evaluate sales by car body style.
- Monitor yearly sales revenue trends.

Dashboard Features

1. Total Car Sold
2. Total Customers
3. Total Sales Revenue
4. Total Sales by Company
5. Monthly/Yearly Sales Revenue
6. Total Sales by Dealer Region
7. Company Filter
8. Total Sales by Dealer
9. Customer by Gender
10. Sales by Car Body Style

Key Insights

- Total sales revenue is approximately 34.43M.
- The customer gender chart shows 313 female and
  305 male customer records.
- California leads among the displayed dealer regions.
- Prime Vehicle Hub has the highest displayed dealer sales.
- Hatchback has the highest displayed sales by body style.
- The yearly sales chart shows an increase from 2024
  to 2025 and a decrease in 2026.
